Tactical FPS game where you try to eliminate the 2 types of AIs( Sniper and pistol). Enemies check different angles and positions that predefined. You have the inital
look of the map where you can see the AIs positions and overall map data. Player have smokes, flashes, nigh vision, leaning with q and e across the walls. Map have 
lights and bushes that lowers or increases your visibivty which can make you harder or easier to detect from AIs. 

W A S D : walking
Ctrl : Crouch
Shift : Run
Q E : Leaning
N : Night vision
M : Map open and close
V : Apply heal
Space : Jump
